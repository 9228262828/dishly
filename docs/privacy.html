DISHLY PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Summary: DISHLY is an offline-first restaurant and dish journal. The current core version does not require an account, Firebase, a backend, cloud sync, ads, analytics, or device location permission.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DISHLY, package com.dishly.foodjournal, handles information when you use the app.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to the current core version of DISHLY and these informational pages.

3. Offline-First Design

DISHLY is designed as an offline-first restaurant and dish journal. The current core features do not require an internet connection.

4. Restaurant Names

Restaurant names entered by you are stored locally on your device.

5. Cuisine Labels

Cuisine information such as Italian, Burgers, Egyptian, or other labels may be stored locally as part of a restaurant entry.

6. Location Notes

DISHLY may store an optional location note entered by you, such as a mall, street, or neighborhood description.

7. No Device Location Access

The current core version does not request precise or approximate device location permission.

8. Location Note Is Manual Text

A location note is text you type manually. It does not give DISHLY access to GPS or device location services.

9. Dish Names

Dish names entered by you are stored locally on your device.

10. Dish Categories

DISHLY may store a selected dish category such as Burger, Pizza, Pasta, Chicken, Seafood, Dessert, Drinks, Breakfast, or Other.

11. Prices

You may optionally save the price you paid or observed for a dish. This value is stored locally.

12. Ratings

DISHLY may store your personal dish rating from the rating control inside the app.

13. Favorites

The app may store whether you marked a dish as a favorite.

14. Would Order Again

The app may store whether you marked a dish as something you would order again.

15. Visit Dates

The app may store the visit date associated with a dish entry.

16. Dish Notes

Free-form notes entered for a dish are stored locally and shown back to you inside the app.

17. Food Journal History

Your restaurant and dish entries together form a local food-journal history on your device.

18. Search

Restaurant and cuisine search is performed locally against information stored in the app.

19. Featured Dish

DISHLY may select a featured dish locally using information such as favorite status and rating.

20. Statistics

DISHLY may calculate local statistics including number of restaurants, number of dishes, favorite count, and average rating.

21. Dark Mode Preference

Your dark-mode preference may be stored locally so the app can remember your appearance choice.

22. Local Storage

The current core version uses local application storage such as SharedPreferences to store saved journal information.

23. No Account Required

You do not need to create a DISHLY account, username, password, or user profile to use the current core version.

24. No Login Data

Because the current core version has no login system, DISHLY does not request sign-in credentials.

25. No Firebase

The current core version is not designed to use Firebase for authentication, analytics, databases, remote configuration, or cloud synchronization.

26. No Remote Backend

The current core version does not send your restaurant or dish journal to a DISHLY-operated backend.

27. No Cloud Sync

The current core version does not provide DISHLY-operated cloud synchronization between devices.

28. No Advertising

The current core version does not contain DISHLY-operated advertising.

29. No Behavioral Analytics

The current core version is not designed to collect behavioral analytics for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

30. No Cross-App Tracking

DISHLY is not designed to track your activity across unrelated third-party apps or websites.

31. No Sale of Personal Information

DISHLY does not intentionally sell or rent the locally stored journal information described in this Policy.

32. Permissions

The current core features do not require access to precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, calendar, photos, media, files, or payment information.

33. Camera

The current core version does not require camera access.

34. Photos and Media

The current core version does not require access to your photo library, gallery, or media library.

35. Microphone

The current core version does not require microphone access or audio recording.

36. Contacts

The current core version does not require access to your contacts.

37. Phone and SMS

The current core version does not require phone-call, call-log, or SMS permissions.

38. Calendar

The current core version does not require access to your device calendar.

39. Files and Documents

The current core version does not require access to personal files or documents for normal use.

40. Payments

The current core version does not require payment-card details, bank credentials, or payment account information.

41. No Food Ordering

The current core version does not place food orders or process food-delivery transactions.

42. No Reservations

The current core version does not create restaurant reservations.

43. No Restaurant Accounts

DISHLY does not log into restaurant websites, restaurant loyalty accounts, or delivery-service accounts in the current core version.

44. No Allergy Data Requirement

DISHLY does not require you to provide allergy, medical, or health information.

45. Sensitive Information

Do not use free-form notes to store passwords, payment credentials, government identifiers, private keys, medical records, or other highly sensitive information.

46. Personal Ratings

Ratings and favorites are your personal journal entries and are stored locally for your own reference.

47. Price Accuracy

Prices saved in DISHLY are values entered by you and may become outdated. DISHLY does not verify restaurant prices.

48. Restaurant Information Accuracy

Restaurant names, cuisine labels, and location notes are user-entered information and are not verified by DISHLY.

49. Data Retention

Locally stored restaurant and dish data generally remains on your device until you delete entries, reset app data, clear application storage, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes the data.

50. Delete Restaurants

Deleting a restaurant from DISHLY removes the restaurant entry and its saved dishes from the app's local data.

51. Delete Dishes

You can delete individual dish entries from a restaurant.

52. Reset All Data

DISHLY includes a reset option that deletes saved restaurants, dishes, favorites, ratings, and statistics from local app storage.

53. Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling DISHLY may remove locally stored information, subject to operating-system or device backup and restore behavior.

54. Device Backups

Your operating system or device provider may back up application data according to your device settings. Such backups are not controlled by DISHLY's core app logic.

55. Data Recovery

Because the current core version does not include DISHLY-operated cloud backup, we may not be able to recover deleted journal entries.

56. Device Security

You are responsible for protecting access to your device using available security features such as a passcode, PIN, password, or biometric lock.

57. Children

DISHLY is a general-purpose personal journal tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children through accounts, messaging, social features, or online profiles.

58. Third-Party Platforms

App stores and device platforms may independently process technical, account, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies.

59. Google Play

If you obtain DISHLY through Google Play, Google may process installation, account, device, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under Google's own policies and terms.

60. GitHub Pages

These informational pages may be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-server information according to its own policies. These pages do not require you to submit DISHLY journal data.

61. Support Communications

If you contact us at mukhtairahmad49@gmail.com, your email provider and our email provider will process the information you voluntarily include. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, secret keys, or other highly sensitive information.

62. Legal Requirements

If legally required, information voluntarily provided through support communications may be disclosed to comply with applicable law or valid legal process.

63. International Use

You may use DISHLY in different countries. Local journal data remains on your device in the current core version, while app stores, email providers, and website hosts may process their own data in other jurisdictions.

64. Future Features

If future releases add accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, restaurant APIs, delivery integrations, reservations, payments, images, precise location, or other permissions, this Policy should be reviewed and updated before release.

65. Your Choices

You can edit or delete restaurant and dish entries, use dark mode, reset all local data, clear app storage, uninstall DISHLY, or stop using the app.

66. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when DISHLY changes or when legal, technical, or app-store requirements change.

67. Contact

For privacy questions about DISHLY, contact mukhtairahmad49@gmail.com.
