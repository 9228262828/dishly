DISHLY — Restaurant & Dish Journal
Tagline: Remember every great bite.
Package: com.dishly.foodjournal

Add to pubspec.yaml:

dependencies:
  flutter:
    sdk: flutter
  shared_preferences: ^2.3.2

Features:
- Splash
- Restaurants
- Cuisine
- Location note (text only)
- Dishes
- Categories
- Price
- Rating
- Favorites
- Would Order Again
- Visit date
- Notes
- Search
- Featured favorite dish
- Statistics
- Dark Mode
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Reset Data
- Offline SharedPreferences storage

No Firebase
No backend
No login
No ads
No analytics
No maps/location permission
No assets required
